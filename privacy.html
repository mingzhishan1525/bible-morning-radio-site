Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Bible Morning Radio ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect and how it is used.

2. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect or store personal data on any server. All video generation happens locally or in your own environment.

When connecting to TikTok via the official Content Posting API, the App requests the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic — Your TikTok user ID and basic profile information, used only to identify your account for publishing.
  • video.publish — Permission to publish videos directly to your TikTok account.
  • video.upload — Permission to upload videos as drafts to your TikTok account.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • TikTok credentials (access tokens) are stored locally on your device and used solely to publish content on your behalf.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third party.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage data beyond what is necessary for the App to function.

4. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally in your environment. No data is transmitted to our servers. You may revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's official API. Please refer to TikTok's Privacy Policy for information about how TikTok handles your data.

6. Your Rights

  • You can revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time by going to TikTok Settings → Manage Apps → Remove the App.
  • You can delete all local tokens by removing the configuration files from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
